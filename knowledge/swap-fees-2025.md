# Swap fee schedule 2025

This schedule applied to simulated swaps during 2025.

## Application fee

Every swap charges an application fee of 0.20% of the amount you spend, taken in the asset you spend.

## Quotes

Quotes are valid for 60 seconds. There is no minimum-received protection; swaps execute at the quoted rate.
