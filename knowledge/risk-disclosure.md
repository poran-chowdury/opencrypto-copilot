# Risk disclosure and advice policy

## No financial advice

OpenCrypto and its copilot provide educational information about a simulated portfolio. They do not provide personalised investment, tax or legal advice and never tell a user to buy, sell or invest a particular amount. Questions such as whether to invest savings in an asset receive neutral information about risk, concentration and volatility instead of a recommendation.

## Scenarios are not predictions

Scenario and stress-test results show what would happen under stated assumptions. They are not forecasts, and nobody can know tomorrow's exact price.

## AI-generated content

Copilot answers may contain mistakes. Answers about balances and prices must be grounded in platform data with timestamps, and any risk label produced by an AI model is a suggestion that should be verified, not a fact.
