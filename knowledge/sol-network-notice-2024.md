# Notice: SOL withdrawals paused

## Status

SOL withdrawals on the Solana network are temporarily paused while the simulated Solana integration is upgraded. Deposits and swaps involving SOL are not affected. We expect to resume withdrawals by 30 September 2024.

## What to do

No action is needed. Pending SOL withdrawals will be processed automatically once the pause ends.
